About

Built from both sides of the divide

Tom, founder of STRIDE

I spent over a decade helping one of the world's largest banks adopt new technology — then built a small business of my own, doing the website, marketing and digital side myself.

It showed me the gap first-hand. Big companies have teams, budgets and specialists to make technology work for them. Small businesses are often left to figure it out themselves.

STRIDE exists to close that gap.

We take the technology big companies take for granted and make it simple, practical and accessible for small businesses — without the jargon, complexity or hassle.

You focus on running your business. We'll make the technology work for you.
