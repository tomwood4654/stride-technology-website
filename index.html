About

Started by someone who's seen both ends of it

I spent over a decade inside one of the world's largest banks, helping thousands of people adopt new technology and actually use it well — including the recent wave of AI. I got to see up close what it looks like when a huge organisation does technology properly: less time wasted, more time spent on the work that actually matters.

At the same time, I built a small business of my own, from nothing, on the side — including building its own website and running its own marketing. It struck me how differently the two worlds work. Large companies have entire teams and unlimited budget to get this right. A one-man plumbing business, a family-run takeaway, the pub on the corner — the businesses that make up most of the country — have none of that. They're just as good at what they do, often better, but they've never had a fair shot at the technology that would make their lives easier.

That's the gap STRIDE exists to close. Small, family-run, local businesses deserve the same access to good technology that large ones have always had — explained simply, with no jargon and no contract you don't understand, from someone who's spent his career making complicated technology work for real people.
