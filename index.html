About

Built from both sides of the divide

Tom, founder of STRIDE

I spent nearly a decade helping one of the world's largest banks adopt new technology — then built a small business of my own and found myself on the other side of the equation.

I was used to seeing teams, budgets and specialists make technology work. Suddenly, I was the one finding the right tools, setting them up and trying to find the time to manage it all myself.

That's when I saw the gap.

Big companies have the resources to make technology work for them. Small businesses are often left to figure it out themselves.

STRIDE exists to close that gap.

We take the technology big companies take for granted and make it simple, practical and accessible for small businesses — without the jargon, complexity or hassle.

You focus on running your business. We'll make the technology work for you.
