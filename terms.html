The free demo

If STRIDE has built a speculative website for your business, it's free and comes with no obligation. Receiving one doesn't create a contract, a fee, or any commitment on your part. If you'd like it taken down, tell us and we will.

Paid services

Anything beyond the free demo — hosting it on your own domain, or any of the services described on our pricing page — is agreed separately with you directly, billed monthly, with no long-term contract. You can cancel at any time.

No guaranteed outcomes

We build and maintain the tools — the website, the booking system, the reminders, and so on. We don't guarantee a specific number of customers, bookings, or amount of revenue; those depend on your business, not just the tools.

What goes into a demo site

Speculative demo sites are built from real, sourced facts about your business (your own reviews, hours, and public details) plus supporting copy written to present them well. We don't invent quotes, credentials, awards, or claims that aren't backed by something real.
