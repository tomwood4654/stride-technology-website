FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Straight answers, no sales patter.

How much does this actually cost?

£45 a month gets you the foundation — a professional website and your Google Business Profile, set up and looked after. Booking, quotes and invoices, and automatic follow-ups are optional add-ons on top, or £110 a month for all four. Full breakdown on the pricing page. No setup fees, no long contracts, cancel any time.

What do you do with my business's information?

We use what's already public about your business — your Google listing, your reviews, your opening hours — to understand how to help. We don't sell or share your information. Full detail in our Privacy Policy.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Everything's month to month. Cancel whenever you like.

I already use Checkatrade, Facebook, or something else — do I still need this?

Probably, alongside it. Those get you found in their own space; this is about your own website, your own Google listing, and customers reaching you directly — not instead of what you already use, as well as it.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Tell us. We'll either put it right or part ways — either way, there's no pressure and nothing you owe us beyond what you've actually used.

Do I need to understand any of the technical side?

No. That's rather the point.

How quickly can I actually get set up?

Depends what you're after, but a basic website and Google listing can usually be sorted within a couple of weeks — get in touch and we'll give you a straight answer for your situation.

What if I already have a website?

That's fine — we can take a look at what you've got and tell you honestly whether it's worth keeping, fixing up, or replacing. No pressure either way.

When do you actually bill me?

Monthly, from whenever you go live — never before you've agreed to it.

Who's actually behind this?

A real person, not a call centre. Read a bit about why we do this, or just message us on WhatsApp.
